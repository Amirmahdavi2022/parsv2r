# Privacy

ParsV2R does not collect anything.

The app talks to the servers you configure and to nothing else, except when you ask it to check
for an update, which reads the releases list of this repository. There is no analytics, no crash
reporting and no account.

Your configurations, your subscriptions and your logs stay on your device. If you send a log to
get help with a problem, you are the one sending it, and it is worth reading it first: a log can
contain server addresses and other details you may not want to share.

ParsV2R is a fork of v2rayNG and inherits its behaviour in this respect.
